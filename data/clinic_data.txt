Common Questions About Professional Dental Cleaning
How soon can I eat after a teeth cleaning?
You can eat right after cleaning, but it’s best to avoid very hard or sticky foods for a few hours.
Does the cleaning whiten teeth permanently?
Cleaning removes surface stains, making teeth appear bright, but it does not provide permanent whitening without additional treatment.
Can teeth cleaning help with bleeding gums?
Removing plaque and tartar reduces gum inflammation which often decreases bleeding during brushing or flossing.
Is teeth cleaning safe during pregnancy?
Yes, professional teeth cleaning is generally safe in all trimesters with proper dental supervision and hygiene.
Can dental cleaning help prevent cavities?
Yes, removing plaque and tartar lowers bacteria buildup, reducing the risk of tooth decay and cavities.
Can children get scaling and polishing safely?
Yes, children can safely receive scaling and polishing under a dentist's supervision to maintain clean teeth and healthy gums.
Are there any side effects after dental cleaning?
Some people may  feel mild gum irritation or temporary tooth sensitivity after cleaning, which usually disappears within a day or two.
Do I need to follow special care before and after my dental cleaning?
Yes, brushing and flossing gently before your appointment helps the dentist work effectively. After cleaning, rinse gently, avoid hard or sticky foods for a few hours, and maintain regular brushing and flossing to keep your teeth healthy and fresh.

Common Queries About Professional Teeth Whitening
Does whitening teeth work on fillings or crowns?
Teeth whitening treatments do not change the color of the crowns, and filling but it restores only the natural color of teeth.
Is teeth whitening suitable for all?
Most adults are eligible, but dentists avoid bleaching during pregnancy or with untreated dental issues. Reach Laksha Care Dental Clinic in Bengaluru if you want more information.
Can yellow teeth become white again?
Yes, professional whitening can significantly improve yellow teeth caused by stains and aging.
Can I eat as usual after bleaching?
Usually, dentists suggest staying away from staining meals and beverages for at least one to two days.
What is the duration for teeth whitening?
Outcome may take a few months up to more than a year depending on the diet, habits, and oral hygiene habits.
In what ways does teeth whitening have side effects?
People might have mild tooth sensitivity or temporary irritation of gums that tend to settle after a few days.
Do teeth whitening have any limitations?
Dentists limit teeth whitening sessions to avoid damaging the enamel and prevent long term sensitivity.
What causes tooth discoloration?
Tooth discoloration can happen due to lifestyle habits like coffee, tea, red wine, or smoking. Certain medications, poor oral hygiene, and aging that thins enamel can also make teeth appear dull or yellow. Identifying the cause helps dentists choose the most effective whitening treatment.

Why Does Your Child Need Pediatric Dental Care?
Children are more likely to get cavities because they enjoy sweets and may miss spots while brushing. Early dental care helps prevent pain and long visits later.
Your child may need treatment if you notice:
Timely care prevents infection and protects the growing permanent teeth.
White or brown spots on teeth
Tooth pain or sensitivity
Swollen or red gums
Difficulty chewing food
Early loss of baby teeth
Food gets stuck often
Types of Treatments We Offer in Pediatric Dentistry
Pediatric Dentistry
These treatments focus on a specific problem or directly protect your child’s teeth.
Types

Common Questions About Children Dental Care & Treatment
At what age should a child first visit a pediatric dentist?
A child should visit a pediatric dentist within 6 months after eruption of the first tooth.
How often should children have dental checkups?
Children should have dental checkups every six months to prevent cavities and detect issues early.
Are dental X-rays safe for children?
Yes, dental X-rays are safe, use minimal radiation, and help detect hidden cavities or developing tooth problems.
Can pediatric dental visits reduce the fear of the dentist?
Yes, early and gentle visits help children feel safe, building trust and reducing dental anxiety.
What foods should children avoid to protect their teeth?
Children should limit sugary snacks, sticky candies, and soft drinks, as these increase the risk of cavities.
What causes tooth decay in children?
Main causes of tooth decay in children include plaque buildup, sugary foods, poor brushing, and bacteria attacking enamel.
What is the best toothpaste for kids?
The best toothpaste for kids is fluoride-based, age-appropriate, and gentle on enamel, ensuring safe cavity prevention.
How much fluoride is safe for kids?
Children under 3 should use a rice-sized amount, and children aged 3–6 should use a pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste.
